INFINIVERSE

Pilot a hovercraft across a twilight island, activate three beacons, and find your way back to the dock.

  • Explore the island

    A 1 × 1 km region with a ridge lookout, ruins, crystal grove, causeway, and starting dock.

  • Follow the ocean

    Travel beyond the coast and the ocean brings you back around to the same island.

  • Make it back

    Avoid the offshore storm and electrical field. Hitting a hazard restarts the run at the dock.

The playable build starts in Phase 2.
